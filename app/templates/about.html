About VeriSight

Designing a Machine Learning-Based Bot for Deepfake Video Detection

The Problem

Digital misinformation powered by AI-generated deepfake videos poses a growing threat to academic integrity and public discourse. University students in Ghana are increasingly exposed to manipulated media that can spread false information, damage reputations, and undermine trust in digital content.

Our Solution

VeriSight is a production-grade deepfake detection platform that leverages state-of-the-art machine learning to analyze videos for signs of AI manipulation. By combining EfficientNet-B0 for frame-level analysis with MTCNN for face detection, VeriSight achieves high accuracy in identifying deepfake content.

Technology Stack

🌐 Python 3.12
⚙ Flask
📚 PyTorch
📷 OpenCV
🗃 MTCNN
📊 EfficientNet
💬 PostgreSQL
☁ Railway

Research Context

This project is a final-year cybersecurity research initiative addressing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal SDG 16 (Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions) by combating misinformation through technological innovation. The target audience is university students in Ghana who face increasing exposure to AI-generated media manipulation.
